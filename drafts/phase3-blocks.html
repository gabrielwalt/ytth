Calories count in alcoholic beverages too

Think twice before taking that next drink.

So, how many calories are in a drink?

Alcohol has a lot of calories, often more than the dessert you turn down. And, having a drink or two before or with a meal may actually stimulate you to eat more and take in more calories. As a result, you may gain extra pounds and add inches to your waistline.

The calorie amounts listed here are for an average-sized drink. Many drinks may have even more, depending on the size of the tumbler, glass or beer mug.
